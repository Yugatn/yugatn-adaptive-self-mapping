# YASMP Metrics v2

Metrics describe the map or its utility. They do not measure the value of a person.

## Sociometric Stability

Measures similarity of network structure across repeated observations.

Possible implementations:

- edge persistence;
- rank correlation of centrality;
- matrix similarity.

## Reciprocity

Proportion of reciprocal relationships under a specified criterion.

## Context Sensitivity

Degree to which a relational or behavioral variable changes between predefined contexts.

## Relational Diversity

Number and distribution of distinct relational positions across groups.

## Symbolic Convergence

Degree to which independently generated symbolic systems produce overlapping propositions.

Important limitation:

Convergence among symbolic systems is not evidence that the systems are independently true. They may share cultural assumptions, structures or interpretive conventions.

## Empirical Convergence

Degree to which a symbolic proposition agrees with independently collected behavioral or sociometric observations.

This is stronger than symbolic convergence.

## Astronomical Incremental Validity

Difference in predictive performance between:

- a baseline model containing ordinary temporal/environmental variables;
- an extended model containing astronomical variables.

Recommended evaluation:

- temporal holdout;
- independent sample;
- out-of-sample loss;
- calibration where predictions are probabilistic.

## Map Utility

Evaluate separately:

- self-understanding;
- communication;
- decision support;
- experimentation;
- development;
- autonomy;
- reciprocity.

Do not collapse these dimensions into a personality score.

## Agency Gain

Difference in perceived and/or observed choice capability after using the map.

A map can be accurate yet harmful if it creates fatalism or typological dependence.

## Typological Risk

Track:

- identity fixation;
- stereotyping;
- discrimination;
- manipulation;
- dependence on the system;
- reduced willingness to revise self-description.

The desired direction is lower risk.
